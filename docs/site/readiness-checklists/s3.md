# Before you migrate S3 {#before-you-migrate-s3}

[← Workload compatibility](../workload-compatibility.md#s3) · [Design gaps](../design-gaps.md#s3)

Answer each question with **yes** or **no**.
If you answer **yes**, read the linked design gap and confirm its workaround
fits your workload before migrating.

1. **Do you need point-in-time restore, delete retention, or account-level recovery to be provisioned automatically through S3 APIs?** → [Account-level recovery and delete-retention features remain operator-provisioned](../design-gaps/s3/account-level-recovery-and-delete-retention-features-remain-operator-provisioned.md)
2. **Does your workload configure lifecycle, replication, website, logging, notifications, acceleration, or Requester Pays through S3 bucket APIs?** → [Bucket sub-resource configs are not translated](../design-gaps/s3/bucket-sub-resource-configs-are-not-translated.md)
3. **Does your workload rely on CompleteMultipartUpload rejecting stale per-part ETags after a part is overwritten?** → [Multipart per-part ETag validation cannot be reproduced](../design-gaps/s3/multipart-per-part-etag-validation-cannot-be-reproduced.md)
4. **Does your workload forbid the proxy from storing any durable multipart-upload control state in Azure?** → [Multipart upload keeps bounded durable proxy state](../design-gaps/s3/multipart-upload-keeps-bounded-durable-proxy-state.md)
5. **Does your workload depend on S3 IAM, bucket policies, or ACLs being enforced by the storage layer?** → [No IAM / ACL / bucket-policy authorization model](../design-gaps/s3/no-iam---acl---bucket-policy-authorization-model.md)
6. **Does your workload require SSE-C, SSE-KMS, or per-request S3 encryption semantics instead of account-level Azure encryption?** → [No enforceable server-side-encryption configuration surface](../design-gaps/s3/no-enforceable-server-side-encryption-configuration-surface.md)
7. **Do you need to add object-lock support later to an existing storage account?** → [Object-lock-capable storage accounts must be provisioned up front](../design-gaps/s3/object-lock-capable-storage-accounts-must-be-provisioned-up-front.md)
8. **During a primary-region outage, must reads automatically fail over to the Azure secondary region?** → [Secondary-region blob reads are not attempted automatically](../design-gaps/s3/secondary-region-blob-reads-are-not-attempted-automatically.md)
