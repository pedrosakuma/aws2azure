# aws2azure v1.1.1 promotion review

> **Release status:** Proposed; stable publication is not authorized by this
> document or by merging its accompanying plans.

The proposed `v1.1.1` promotion reuses the exact `v1.1.1-rc.5` candidate at
`bf2274dc6469ce2a8a0c2db4b956391f24c74076`. It must not rebuild the Native AOT
archives or container images. The [RC5 review](v1.1.1-rc.5.md) and
[canonical manifest](v1.1.1-rc.5-manifest.json) retain the exact artifact
identities, four observation receipts, and interpretation limits.

## Proposed scope

The four workload profiles are `s3-basic-object-crud`,
`secretsmanager-basic-lifecycle`, `dynamodb-basic-crud`, and
`sqs-standard-messaging`, each version 1. All completed their dedicated
60-minute dual-cohort real-Azure observations against the unchanged RC5 bytes.
This is workload-scoped evidence, not full AWS service parity. Consult the
[live certification](../site/workload-ga.md) and per-operation gap docs before
adoption; this proposal does not override their current status.

Shipping changes since `v1.1.0` include cached AMQP CBS authorization renewal
([#1048](https://github.com/pedrosakuma/aws2azure/pull/1048)), sanitized
SecretsManager authentication diagnostics
([#1057](https://github.com/pedrosakuma/aws2azure/pull/1057)), DynamoDB batch
encoder allocation changes, and Entra token-endpoint retry backoff.
Sanitized diagnostics do not establish the cause of the historical
SecretsManager observation failure.

## Review boundaries

The [promotion plan](v1.1.1-promotion.json) selects immutable archive, GHCR,
and observation artifacts. Its [readiness plan](v1.1.1-readiness.json) must
pass the live, read-only exact-run gate before any publication. A passing
readiness receipt is not permission to publish.

The committed observation policies use absolute thresholds. DynamoDB and S3
historical candidate/prior throughput differences remain unexplained; passing those
thresholds does not establish performance parity. SecretsManager passed the
existing 4.5 observation override, not the qualification floor of 9.
The concerns recorded in [#1016](https://github.com/pedrosakuma/aws2azure/issues/1016)
and the [RC5 review](v1.1.1-rc.5.md) must remain visible in the promotion
decision. No baseline, policy, or threshold is changed by these plans.

The historical current-freshness boundaries remain unchanged: the first
qualification boundary was `2026-09-29T23:56:59.1385758Z` (SQS), and the first
observation boundary was `2026-10-01T14:10:59.5815164Z`.
The [per-release evidence policy](../testing/release-evidence-decisions.md)
separates original validity from current release eligibility instead of requiring
full renewal solely because 72 hours elapsed. The
[RC5 evidence decision](v1.1.1-evidence-decision.json) records the per-profile
assessment below. Its acceptance is limited to evidence reuse for these exact
bytes; it is **not publication authorization**. No evidence timestamps, floors
or canonical measurements are changed.

## Evidence reuse assessment (2026-10-02)

**Disposition: reuse all four profiles; no additional paid check selected.**
The assessment was prepared by Copilot at the operator's request and is subject
to review/merge with the input-bound decision. This is not a claim of current
Azure capacity or a new measurement of every deployment's health.

The reviewed range `bf2274dc..f8c7ac87` contains no shipping-source or root
build/dependency change. From archive approval `bd99630d` onward, the four
ledgers, canonical qualifications, observation policies and gap contracts are
unchanged. The authority implementation hash changed for #1077; its evaluation
instant and canonical-input revision did not. The selected persisted-format
inventory and attested prior remain unchanged.

The later test/orchestration changes do not invalidate those claims:
#1059 fixed observation assembly before the four selected successful runs;
#1063/#1064/#1065/#1069/#1071 added or repaired report-only diagnostics;
#1067 refreshed runner OIDC assertions; #1073 fixed diagnostic cleanup login;
#1075/#1076 added the S3 comparison and corrected its preflight. Explicitly
disabling S3 versioning in the future provisioning template does not rewrite
the original basic-CRUD evidence or claim that it tested versioning. #1077
changed the promotion decision policy, not the original evidence rules.

The open-issue review found no unresolved **evidence-invalidating** incident
in the inspected repository reports. That does not close #1016, explain the
historical SecretsManager 403, or assert access to private Azure service-health
telemetry. The passing canonical lifecycle/rotation evidence for the exact
candidate supports accepting that residual auth uncertainty for this scope;
non-recurrence in a diagnostic alone would not prove a fix. Later PR Azure
integration/load runs `36936125314/1` and `36936125345/1` passed, but they use
their own PR identities and are not relabeled as RC5 qualification or required
current-health checks. No replacement 72-hour deadline is introduced.

Read-only reassessment downloaded and verified all ten archive/GHCR/observation
input ZIPs, reproduced the exact RC identity and final manifest, validated all
four qualifications/observations at their original issuance, and passed the
exact-run readiness gate. Source availability is checked again by the decision
gate and at publication; this assessment never extends retention.

Reopen the decision for a relevant defect or authentication recurrence, changed
runtime/profile/backend/auth contract, changed rollback target, invalidated
harness assertion, revoked identity or unavailable artifact. Uncertainty about
the impact blocks reuse until reviewed. An actual current capacity claim would
need appropriately scoped new measurements and separate spending authorization.
Otherwise, elapsed time alone is not a reason to repeat the complete campaign.

Native archive filenames and checksums remain those in the RC5 manifest.

## Publication decision

of the documented limitations, and explicit operator authorization may
`release-candidate-promote.yml` be dispatched from protected `main` with the
exact merged orchestration SHA and `docs/releases/v1.1.1-promotion.json`.

followed by publication. Run `eng/release-readiness-gate.py gate` separately
for a read-only check.
